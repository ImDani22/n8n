1. Overview

N8N YouTube Studio is a self-hosted content-production and publishing application. It allows its owner to connect supported social-media accounts, create video content, and publish content to accounts that the owner has explicitly authorized.

2. Information we may process

  • Basic account or profile information made available by the platform.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.
  • Account, channel or profile identifiers.
  • Publishing permissions granted by the user.
  • Publication status and basic performance metrics when supported.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested functionality such as connecting accounts, publishing user-selected content, checking publication status, and displaying account information in the management panel.

4. Storage

The application is designed to run in a self-hosted environment controlled by the app owner. Connection data and OAuth tokens are stored in that environment to maintain authorized account connections. The application does not sell personal information.

5. Sharing

Information is shared only with the relevant platform when necessary to perform an action requested by the user. We do not sell or rent connected-account data.

6. Retention and deletion

The user can disconnect or remove a connected account from the application, which removes locally stored connection credentials used by this application. This does not delete the user's account on the external platform.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit unnecessary exposure of credentials and account data.

8. Third-party platforms

Use of external platforms such as TikTok is also subject to those platforms' own privacy policies and terms.
